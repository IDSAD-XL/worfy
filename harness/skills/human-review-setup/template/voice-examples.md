# Voice examples — the team's own comments by tier

Real review comments of this team, by tier, each with its link. Read the examples of a tier before writing a
comment of that tier: match their length and their temperature, not their words. Never copy a sentence.

How this file is kept, by the generator and by a person who edits it by hand:

- Each example is a link to the comment, the reviewer's label and the length, and the comment's own text below
  it as a quote, as written: lines the comment itself quoted (`>`) are left out.
- No login anywhere: a reviewer is `reviewer A`, `reviewer B`, … as in `research.md`, and a mention of anyone
  in a quote becomes `@someone`.
- Up to 6 examples per tier, from different areas of code where the history has them.
- When the examples of a tier come from three or more reviewers, no reviewer gives more than a third of them.
  With one or two reviewers in a tier, a line under its heading says so.
- A tier with no comments in the window says "No comments of this tier in the window."

The format of one example:

```markdown
- [<owner>/<repo>#<number>](<link to the comment>) — reviewer B, 84 chars
  > the comment's own text, as written
```
